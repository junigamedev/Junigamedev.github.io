Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2024
Last Updated: May 20, 2024

Welcome to junigamedev (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile games.

By playing our games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on which game you play, we and our service providers collect certain information automatically or directly from you:

  • Device & Identifier Data: Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address (used to estimate coarse location), device model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information about ads served, views, taps, and performance metrics.
  • Gameplay Data: Achievements, high scores, crash logs, and session durations.
  • In-App Purchase Data (Dungeon Legacy): Payment receipts and order IDs handled via the Google Play Store. We never store raw credit card or financial details.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising Partners

Our games use Google AdMob to serve in-game advertisements.

Google AdMob uses device identifiers and local storage technologies to deliver, personalize, measure, and analyze ad performance. You can learn more about how Google processes data collected through our games by visiting Google’s Privacy & Terms Page.

  • Rewarded & Standard Ads: Uses Google AdMob to serve standard and rewarded video ads (where you can choose to view an ad for in-game items or perks).
  • In-Game Purchases (IAP): Offers virtual items and currency for purchase. Payment verification is securely handled through the Google Play Store.

B. Feudal

  • Advertising: Uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, or video ads.

C. Crown Siege

  • Advertising: Uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, or video ads.

D. Marble Maw

  • Advertising: Uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, or video ads.

E. Eonfall

  • Advertising: Uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, or video ads.

F. Ember & Anvil

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our games are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain European regions) without consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately to have it deleted.

5. Your Choices & Ad Preferences

  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID on your Android device through system settings:
    • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID).
  • Consent Options (EEA/UK Users): If you reside in the European Economic Area or UK, you will be presented with an in-app consent prompt (Consent Management Platform) allowing you to manage your ad choices.
